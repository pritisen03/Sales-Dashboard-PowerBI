# 📊 Sales Performance Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive Power BI dashboard that analyzes sales performance, profit margins,
product-line trends and customer distribution across countries.

![Dashboard Preview](images/dashboard.png)

## 🎯 Objective
To help business stakeholders understand sales trends, identify top and bottom
performing products, and make data-driven decisions.

## 📈 Key Metrics (KPIs)
| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Total Sales | $9.60M |
| Profit Margin | $3.83M |
| Profit Margin % | 39.8% |
| Top Selling Product | 1992 Ferrari 360 Spider red |
| 2004 Monthly Sales vs Goal | $0.43M vs $0.28M (+54.97%) |

## 🔍 Key Insights
- **2004 was the best year** with $4.5M in sales, compared to $3.3M in 2003.
- **Classic Cars** contribute the highest share (40.13%) of sales, followed by
  Vintage Cars (18.72%).
- **Trains** are the lowest-selling product line (~$0.2M).
- The **1992 Ferrari 360 Spider red** is the top product with $0.28M in sales.
- Customers are mainly concentrated in **Europe and North America**.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- Power BI Desktop
- DAX (measures and calculated columns)
- Power Query (data cleaning and transformation)
- Excel / CSV (data source)

## 📊 Dashboard Features
- KPI cards (Total Sales, Profit Margin, Top Product)
- Slicers: Order Year, City, Product Line, Month
- Sales by Year (bar chart)
- Sales by Product Line (pie chart)
- Customer Distribution by Country (map)
- Top 5 and Bottom 5 selling products
- Quantity Sold by Product Line (treemap)
- Drill-through pages for product and customer details

## 📁 Project Structure
- `Sales_Dashboard.pbix` – Power BI file
- `Sales_Dashboard.pdf` – Exported dashboard
- `images/` – Dashboard screenshots

## 🚀 How to Use
1. Download the `.pbix` file.
2. Open it in Power BI Desktop.
3. Use the slicers to filter by year, city, product line or month.
